# Welcome to the Fabric Wiki!
This resource is dedicated to Fabric. Fabric is a modular modding toolchain targeting Minecraft: Java Edition 1.14 and above, including snapshots.

Information on the wiki is community contributed. Some pages on the wiki may be out-of-date or a work in progress. For official, properly versioned documentation, try the https://docs.fabricmc.net/|Fabric Docs.
