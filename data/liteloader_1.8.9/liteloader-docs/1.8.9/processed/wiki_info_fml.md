# forge modloader (fml)
Early versions of [Minecraft Forge](forge) were built on [ModLoader](ModLoader) by [people:Risugami](people:Risugami) as the mod loading technology, since it was the mod loading platform of choice at the time. Multiplayer mod support was provided by [ModLoaderMP](ModLoaderMP) by [ScottyDoesKnow (SDK)](people:sdk). In early 2012 it was decided to switch to an in-house mod loading solution which would (at least at first) be backward-compatible with [ModLoader](ModLoader) but would incorporate the functionality provided by [ModLoaderMP](ModLoaderMP) and remove the dependency on the third-party projects. **FML** was developed by [people:cpw](people:cpw) to fulfill this role.

**FML** maintained a high level of compatibility with [ModLoader](ModLoader) mods until *Minecraft 1.7*, where the ModLoader baggage was finally jettisoned in favour of **FML**'s more flexible and advanced mod platform.

See also: [FML on GitHub](https://github.com/MinecraftForge/FML)
