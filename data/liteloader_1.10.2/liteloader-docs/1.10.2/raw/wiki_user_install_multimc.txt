Installing LiteLoader with MultiMC
==================================

[MultiMC](http://multimc.org/) has built-in support for LiteLoader and installing LiteLoader is extremely simple. To add LiteLoader to a MultiMC instance, take the following steps:

* Click the **Edit Instance** button to show the instance editor dialogue
 {{:user:install:mmc1.jpg?nolink&300|}}
* Click the **Install LiteLoader** button, you will be presented with a dialogue showing the available versions
 {{:user:install:mmc2.jpg?nolink&240|}}
* LiteLoader will appear in the list, indicating it was installed successfully
 {{:user:install:mmc3.jpg?nolink&300|}}

That's it! LiteLoader is now installed successfully with MultiMC.
