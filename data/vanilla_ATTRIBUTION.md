# Vanilla registry dumps

Registry ID lists under `data/vanilla_*/registries/` may be built from:

- PrismarineJS/minecraft-data (MIT): https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data
- Official Minecraft `reports/` from the data generator

Mojang registry IDs themselves are game content names.
