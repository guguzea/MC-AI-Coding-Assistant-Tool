Installing LiteLoader
=====================

As a [[info:Tweak]], LiteLoader supports several different installation methods in order to accomodate a variety of environments, the simplest method being to use the [[user:install:installer|LiteLoader Installer]]. If you're not sure what method to use, follow the [[user:install:trail|Installation Trail]].

* Using the [[user:install:installer|LiteLoader Installer]]
* Installation if you are using [[user:install:multimc|MultiMC]]
* Installing [[user:install:manual|manually]]
* Installing when you [[user:install:forge|already have Minecraft Forge or FML installed]]

LiteLoader also supports being installed along with other Tweaks such as [[http://www.minecraftforum.net/forums/mapping-and-modding/minecraft-mods/1272953-optifine|Optifine]] and [[http://www.minecraftforum.net/forums/mapping-and-modding/minecraft-mods/1286604-shaders-mod|Shaders Mod]], see the link below for instructions:

* [[user:install:othertweaks|Installing other Tweaks mods (eg. Optifine, ShadersMod)]]
